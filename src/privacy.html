Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. 100% Client-Side Architecture

BWTools.uk provides free, privacy-first web utilities. All file processing—including AI background removal, image compression, image cropping, and PDF compilation/splitting—occurs exclusively inside your local web browser using WebAssembly, JavaScript, and HTML5 Canvas APIs.

Your documents, photographs, and personal files are never transmitted, uploaded, or stored on our servers or any third-party cloud infrastructure.

2. Cookies & Advertising

We use third-party advertising services (such as Google AdSense) to serve ads when you visit our website. These companies may use cookies, web beacons, and similar identifiers to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites on the Internet.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to BWTools.uk and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or through aboutads.info.

3. Analytics & Local Storage

We use local storage strictly on your browser device to save user preferences, such as your selected dark or light display theme. This preference data remains strictly within your browser and is never sold or shared.

4. Third-Party Links

Our site contains links to external services (such as Buy Me a Coffee and developer tools). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external third-party sites.

5. Contact Information

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy or tool functionality, you can contact us via our About & Contact page.
