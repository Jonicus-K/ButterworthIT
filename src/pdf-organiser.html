100% Client-Side Privacy

Unlike standard PDF services that upload your confidential files to remote cloud servers, BW Tools renders and modifies documents entirely within your browser using HTML5 Canvas, Web Workers, and WebAssembly. Sensitive IDs, contracts, and financial statements never leave your local device.

Lossless Vector Assembly

When reorganising or rotating pages, the tool parses the low-level PDF document dictionary and updates the transformation matrix without recompressing text or embedded vector shapes. This preserves the original resolution, typography, and interactive form fields.
